Observation Journal

A practical approach to noticing patterns, reflecting on experience, and connecting observations to larger ideas.

Practice

Observe with curiosity. Connect with purpose.

An Observation Journal is a practical tool for noticing what is happening before deciding what it means.

It can help turn everyday experiences into questions, insights, and new connections.
