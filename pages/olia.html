Organizational Learning Infrastructure Assessment

Explore OLIA and the organizational practices that support learning, knowledge, reflection, and adaptation.

Assessment

Examine the infrastructure that supports organizational learning

The Organizational Learning Infrastructure Assessment (OLIA) is a diagnostic tool designed to help leaders understand how effectively their organization detects change, captures knowledge, learns from experience, and adapts over time.

It examines the systems and practices that enable organizational learning across ten domains:

  1. Environmental Sensemaking: Detecting emerging internal and external changes before they become crises.
  2. Knowledge Capture: Preserving knowledge before it is lost.
  3. Reflection: Turning experience into insight.
  4. Decision Learning: Improving future judgment by reviewing important decisions.
  5. Experimentation: Testing assumptions through disciplined learning.
  6. Cross-Boundary Learning: Moving knowledge across organizational boundaries.
  7. Feedback Integration: Using stakeholder feedback to improve performance.
  8. Capability Development: Building workforce capability.
  9. Organizational Memory: Retaining institutional knowledge.
  10. Adaptation & Scaling: Spreading successful practices and retiring ineffective ones.

OLIA helps leaders identify strengths and gaps in the infrastructure that supports organizational learning. Rather than focusing solely on outcomes or individual performance, it examines whether the organization has the processes, practices, and mechanisms needed to turn information and experience into improved decisions and sustained adaptation.

Its purpose is to help leaders strengthen the conditions for continuous learning, resilience, and long-term organizational effectiveness.
