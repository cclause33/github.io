What the assessment measures

The Team Health Assessment is a diagnostic tool designed to help teams understand how effectively they work, perform, learn, and adapt. It measures five dimensions of team health:

  • Clarity: How well team members understand their purpose, priorities, roles, and expectations.
  • Performance: How consistently the team follows through on commitments, manages resources, and executes its work.
  • Adaptability: How effectively the team identifies problems, responds to feedback, and learns from experience.
  • Resilience: How well the team navigates setbacks, manages pressure, supports its members, and maintains momentum through uncertainty.
  • Leadership: How effectively leadership provides direction, listens to different perspectives, encourages open dialogue, and supports continuous improvement.

The assessment combines individual ratings with open-ended reflection questions to develop a shared picture of team strengths and opportunities for improvement. Its purpose is not to evaluate individual employees, but to help teams identify patterns, strengthen collaboration, and determine where focused action could improve team effectiveness.
